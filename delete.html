Delete Account or Data

Kisogo. Account and data deletion.

You can delete your Kisogo account or specific data at any time. Most actions can be completed directly inside the app.

Delete Your Entire Account

Deleting your account permanently removes all your data from our systems.

  1. Open the Kisogo app on your device.
  2. Tap the menu icon at the top left of the screen.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings screen.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Confirm your decision when prompted.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed.

  • Your account credentials and email address.
  • All vocabulary cards you have created.
  • Your study history and review logs.
  • Your app preferences and settings.
  • Your streak and daily goal data.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

Delete Specific Data Without Deleting Your Account

You can delete individual vocabulary cards at any time without deleting your account.

  1. Open the Kisogo app on your device.
  2. Tap the menu icon at the top left of the screen.
  3. Tap Library.
  4. Find the card you want to delete.
  5. Tap the three dots icon in the top right corner of the card.
  6. Tap Delete card.

Deleted cards and their associated images are removed from our systems immediately. Your account, study history, and other cards remain unaffected.

Data Retention

All account data is deleted immediately upon account deletion confirmation. Individual card data is deleted immediately when a card is removed. No data is retained after deletion. Images submitted for analysis were processed by OpenAI and are subject to their data retention policies, which you can review at openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.
