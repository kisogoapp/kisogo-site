Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a vocabulary card?

Open the app and point your camera at any object. Tap the shutter button, then tap "Use This." The app will identify the object and generate a vocabulary card in your target language automatically.

What if the app identifies the wrong object?

After the app identifies the object, you will see a "Wrong? Fix label" option before the vocabulary is generated. Tap it, type the correct name, and the card will be created with the right vocabulary.

How do I upload a photo from my gallery?

Tap the menu icon at the top left and select "Upload from gallery." Choose a photo, then use the crop tool to select the object you want to learn.

How does the study system work?

Kisogo uses spaced repetition. Cards you find difficult appear more frequently, while cards you know well appear less often. This is the most effective method for long-term vocabulary retention.

Can I change my target language?

Yes. Go to Menu, then Settings, then Target language, and select a new language. Note that existing cards will remain in your previous language.

How do I delete a card?

Go to Menu, then Library. Find the card, tap the three dots icon in the top right corner of the card, and select "Delete card."

How do I delete my account?

Go to Menu, then Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your account and all associated data.
